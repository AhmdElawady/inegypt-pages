1. Description of Service

InEgypt is a transit routing and travel companion app for Egypt. It provides:

  • Public transit route planning (Metro, BRT, LRT, Monorail, Tram, Train, intercity Bus, flights)
  • Station, line, and intercity-route information
  • Attraction, city, and category guides
  • An interactive map with custom places categories
  • A trip planner that builds day-by-day itineraries from curated and Apple Maps data
  • A trip expense tracker with multi-currency support
  • A standalone currency converter
  • Weather information for trip cities (via Apple WeatherKit)
  • Safety information (emergency numbers, scam awareness, fair pricing guides)
  • Links to third-party tour operators (e.g. GetYourGuide) where you can book activities

The app is intended as an informational and planning tool to assist with navigation and travel in Egypt.

The trip planner generates itineraries algorithmically from curated InEgypt data and Apple Maps points of interest. Suggestions are informational only and do not guarantee that a place is open, available, accessible, safe, or appropriate for your situation. Always verify opening hours, ticket prices, and conditions directly with the venue or operator before traveling.

The expense tracker is a personal recordkeeping tool. It is not financial, accounting, or tax advice. Currency exchange rates shown in the app and converter come from a third-party data provider (ExchangeRate-API) and from cached or fallback values when the network is unavailable. Rates shown are estimates only and may differ from the rate your bank or card issuer applies. Always verify the actual rate before making financial decisions.

Weather data comes from Apple WeatherKit and may be inaccurate, delayed, or unavailable for some cities. Multi-day forecasts are limited to roughly the next 10 days. Do not rely on InEgypt weather for safety-critical decisions.
