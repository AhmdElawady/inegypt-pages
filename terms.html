1. Description of Service

InEgypt is a transit routing and travel companion app for Egypt. It provides:

  • Public transit route planning (Metro, BRT, LRT, Monorail, Tram, Train, intercity Bus)
  • Station, line and intercity-route information
  • Attraction and city guides, and an interactive map
  • A trip planner that builds day-by-day plans from curated InEgypt data and Apple Maps places, or checks a plan you bring
  • Trip help such as a before-you-fly checklist and an arrival guide
  • A trip expense tracker and a currency converter
  • Weather for the cities and days of your trips (via Apple WeatherKit)
  • Safety information (emergency numbers, scam awareness, fair pricing guides)
  • Links to official ticket sellers and to partner tours (for example GetYourGuide)

The app is intended as an informational and planning tool to assist with navigation and travel in Egypt.

5. Trip Planner, Expenses & Other Planning Tools

The trip planner builds plans from curated InEgypt data and Apple Maps places. Suggestions are informational only and do not guarantee that a place is open, available, accessible, safe, or appropriate for your situation. Always verify opening hours, ticket prices, and conditions directly with the venue or operator before traveling.

The expense tracker is a personal recordkeeping tool. It is not financial, accounting, or tax advice. Currency exchange rates come from a third-party data provider (ExchangeRate-API) and from cached or fallback values when the network is unavailable. Rates shown are estimates only and may differ from the rate your bank or card issuer applies.

Weather data comes from Apple WeatherKit and may be inaccurate, delayed, or unavailable for some cities. Multi-day forecasts are limited to roughly the next 10 days. Do not rely on InEgypt weather for safety-critical decisions.
