Good to know

Questions, answered

Does InEgypt work offline?

Yes. Routes, maps, city and attraction guides, line details and safety info all live on your device after download. A connection is only needed to refresh things like live currency rates.

Which transit systems and cities are covered?

InEgypt plans across Metro, Train, Monorail, BRT, LRT, Tram and intercity bus, train and flights. Cairo and Alexandria local buses are included as a helpful fallback — useful, but not as complete or current as the rail modes. It covers 34 cities and 220+ attractions.

Is InEgypt free?

Yes — it's free to download and explore cities, attractions, the map and the trip planner. Pro unlocks unlimited routing, full offline access and the travel-smart tools.

Can I use it before arriving in Egypt?

Absolutely. Plan your whole trip in advance and download everything for offline use, so it's ready the moment you land.

What does Pro unlock?

Unlimited route planning, full offline access, line details and timetables, Scam Shield, the fair-price guide and Get Ready. Choose Monthly or Yearly (Yearly adds Family Sharing), a one-time Lifetime purchase, or a 7-day Trip Pass for a visit.
